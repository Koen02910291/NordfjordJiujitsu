About Jiu-Jitsu

Jiu-jitsu, also written as ju-jitsu and jujutsu, is a versatile martial art that includes strikes, kicks, grips, throws, and self-defence techniques. It has its roots in traditional Japanese martial arts and has evolved in Europe into a modern and effective self-defence system.

Smart technique

Use technique, balance, timing, and body mechanics to handle attacks safely and effectively, even against larger and stronger opponents.

Varied training

Sessions include warm-ups, technical practice, and physical development. Resistance, stress, and scenario-based work are introduced gradually.

For every level

Suitable for complete beginners and people with previous martial arts experience. Learn practical self-defence while improving fitness.

More About Jiu-Jitsu

History, training and our tradition

Jiu-jitsu is a modern and versatile martial art that includes strikes, kicks, grappling, throws, breakfalls, joint locks, and escape techniques. Although its roots lie in traditional Japanese martial arts, it has evolved in Europe under the influence of boxing, wrestling, and other combat disciplines.

You learn to use an opponent's strength and movement to your advantage through technique, timing, balance, and body mechanics. Training consists of warm-ups, technical drills, practical exercises, sparring, and scenario-based training.

Our club is built on a European jiu-jitsu tradition that traces its roots to the Japanese pioneers who introduced jujutsu to Europe in the early 1900s. The style has been refined through several generations of instructors, with a focus on practical self-defence and effective teaching.

We create an inclusive environment where training is adapted to the participant's level and goals. Everyone is warmly welcome to join.
