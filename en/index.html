About Jiu-Jitsu

Jiu-jitsu (also written as ju-jitsu and jujutsu) is a versatile martial art that includes strikes, kicks, grips, throws, and self-defence techniques. It has its roots in traditional Japanese martial arts and has evolved in Europe into a modern and effective self-defence system.

You learn to use technique, balance, timing, and body mechanics to handle attacks safely and effectively, even against larger and stronger opponents.

Training sessions include warm-up exercises, technical practice, and physical development. As your fundamental techniques improve, we gradually introduce resistance, stress, and scenario-based training. At the same time, you develop strength, coordination, fitness, balance, and flexibility.

Jiu-jitsu is suitable for all ages and experience levels, whether you are a complete beginner or have experience from other martial arts. You learn practical self-defence while improving your overall fitness.

More About Jiu-Jitsu

Jiu-jitsu (also written as ju-jitsu and jujutsu) is a modern and versatile martial art that includes strikes, kicks, grappling, throws, breakfalls, joint locks, and escape techniques. Although its roots lie in the traditional Japanese martial arts of the samurai, it has evolved in Europe under the influence of boxing, wrestling, and other combat disciplines. The result is a diverse martial art with a strong focus on effective self-defence.

In jiu-jitsu, you learn to use your opponent's strength and movement to your advantage through technique, timing, balance, and body mechanics. Training consists of warm-ups, technical drills, practical exercises, sparring, and scenario-based training. You build strength, fitness, coordination, balance, and self-confidence while learning skills that can be applied in both self-defence and competition.

Our club is built on a European jiu-jitsu tradition that traces its roots back to the Japanese pioneers who introduced jujutsu to Europe in the early 1900s. Through several generations of instructors, the style has been refined with a focus on practical self-defence, effective teaching methods, and techniques that work for practitioners of all ages and experience levels.

We place great importance on creating an inclusive environment where everyone feels welcome. Whether you are a complete beginner or have previous martial arts experience, training is adapted to your level and personal goals. With us, you will find a friendly and social group, varied training sessions, and a strong sense of community. We guarantee an intensive, challenging, and enjoyable workout every time.

Everyone is warmly welcome to join us and discover this fascinating and rewarding martial art together with us!
